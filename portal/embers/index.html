Nightlife series · Geneva · since 2021

We have been throwing raves around Geneva since 2021, and every November the night calls us back.

In January 2025, EMBERS the RAVE took over Sonic Meteor, from 10pm to 6am, with about 300 people on the floor.

From Dirty Downtempo to Hi-Tech and everything that lurks in between.

A journey through exquisite soundscapes and delirious beats for you to let go.

The crowd comes ready to sweat, expressive dancers among them, the kind that get a whole floor moving.

Every night so far has been prix libre: pay what you want.
