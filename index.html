Next Tuesday Society

We meet every Tuesday. The rest grows from there.

We support artistic and philosophical initiatives of a mythopoetic nature. Shared stories and fictions, taken seriously, as a way to build meaningful relationships, to engage with the material world, and to bring desirable futures a little closer.

In practice: nights, gatherings and long conversations. Aesthetic and transformative experiences, and spaces for shared presence, nuanced dialogue and experimentation. We run on do-ocracy, self-organization and mutual care.
