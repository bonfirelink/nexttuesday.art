Tuesdays

Yes. We gather every Tuesday, of all days, and in Geneva, of all places. It has been so for years and years.

It is a ritual. A simple one. Just show up. Share a meal. Share a story. Share a dream. Find the others.

We expect you. And you'll be most welcome.

Come next Tuesday. We'll conspire.

> INTERSECT

An ancient tradition, a crossroads of sorts

And then there is everything else. A crossroad of mystical traditions, embodied practices, hacker culture, speculative realism, art and philosophy, at the fringes of a society that is yet to come.

INTERSECT, an assembly at the Chaos Communication Congress, is one of the paths through it.
