INTERSECT

An ancient tradition, a crossroads where humans stop and gather to reflect.

INTERSECT is an assembly at the Chaos Communication Congress, held every year between Christmas and New Year. Talks, workshops, conversations and live music: a serious playground for weaving stories of possible futures in times of apocalypse.

It is an initiative in our orbit, close to what we care about: shared stories as a way of meeting the future together.
