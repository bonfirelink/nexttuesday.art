On everything, nothing and anything in between

Philo salon

The philo salon format is an intimate space for collective inquiry and shared reflection. The event unfolds through short provocations and evolving constellations of dialogue, giving each voice space to find its place in the flow of conversation. In the background, a low hum of music and soft surroundings hold us in the rhythm of thinking together.

No prior knowledge required, only curiosity, a willingness to think out loud and a spirit of respectful engagement.

Conversations move between English and French.
