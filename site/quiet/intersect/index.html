> INTERSECT

An ancient tradition, a crossroads where humans stop and gather to reflect.

In the endless field

An assembly at the Chaos Communication Congress, the hacker gathering held between Christmas and New Year. Talks, workshops, conversations and live music.

It is said that those who come leave transformed by each other's perspectives, walking towards shared futures worth materializing.

The vision: a serious playground for weaving stories of possible futures in times of apocalypse.

They cometh not alone.
