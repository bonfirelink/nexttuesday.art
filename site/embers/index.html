EMBERS

The floor ain't gonna dance itself.

EMBERS is where our community dances. We have been throwing raves in Geneva since 2021, with one every November, and EMBERS the RAVE at Sonic Meteor in January 2025, with around 300 people on the floor.

The music moves around: bass house, psytechno, drum & bass, hardgroove... What stays the same is the crowd. Our people come to dance, expressive dancers among them, and they get a floor moving.

So far, every night has been prix libre: pay what you want.

For venues

If you have a room and a sound system that deserve a night like this, write to us at hello@nexttuesday.art.
