Philo salon

The philo salon format is an intimate space for collective inquiry and shared reflection.

The event unfolds through short provocations and evolving constellations of dialogue, giving each voice space to find its place in the flow of conversation. In the background, a low hum of music and soft surroundings hold us in the rhythm of thinking together.

No prior knowledge required, only curiosity, a willingness to think out loud and a spirit of respectful engagement.

Conversations move between English and French.

The volumes so far

  1. Vol. 1 · November 2025

    A collective inquiry onDo-ocracy

    What happens when doing becomes the way we decide?

    A mode of organization rooted in open-source communities, hackerspaces, and the Burning Man scene where initiative itself grants authority.

  2. Vol. 2 · February 2026

    A collective inquiry onthe power ofFictions

    How do certain stories come to define the reality we inhabit?

    Building worlds in an age of collapse one story at a time. Who gets to write and circulate them in the age of AI?

  3. Vol. 3 · March 2026

    A collective inquiry onthe limits ofLanguage

    Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent?

    Or does understanding sometimes begin precisely where language falls silent?

All three at La Galerie, Rue de l'Industrie 13, 1201 Geneva.
